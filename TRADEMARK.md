# Keel trademark policy

Keel's code is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](./LICENSE). You're free to use it, change it, and run it.

The **Keel name and logo** (the sun, hull, waterline, and keel mark) are different. They tell people they're using the real Keel, built by this project and held to its [Manifesto](./MANIFESTO.md). For people in one of the hardest moments of their working lives, knowing who they're trusting matters.

## What's fine without asking

- Saying your project is "based on Keel" or "a fork of Keel," with a link back to this repository.
- Running Keel, unchanged or lightly configured, for your library, union, school, nonprofit, or workforce program, and calling it Keel.
- Writing about Keel, teaching with it, and linking to it.

## What needs permission

- Using "Keel" or the Keel logo as the name or logo of a **modified** version, a fork, or a different product or service.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests the Keel project endorses you, your product, or your organization.
- Using the name or logo on merchandise or in advertising.

If you fork Keel and change it, please give your version its own name and logo.

To ask for permission, open an issue in this repository.

*This policy may change as Keel grows. It is not legal advice.*
