# The Keel Manifesto

AI is changing work faster than people can change with it. Jobs are disappearing. Others are being hollowed out, with the title intact but the parts that made the work *yours* handed to a machine. For many of us, work was more than a paycheck. It was how we answered the question *"Who are you?"*

Keel exists for the moment that answer stops working.

## What we believe

**1. Humans first, AI second.**
Keel uses AI as a tool in service of a person, never the other way around. When there's a tradeoff between what's efficient and what's humane, we choose humane.

**2. You are more than your job.**
We start with your values, your relationships, and what you care about outside of work. A career is one way to live out who you are. It is not who you are.

**3. Meaningful work is a human need.**
People don't just want employment. They want work that matters. Keel's goal is a vocation: work that lines up with your values.

**4. The AI reflects. You decide.**
Keel's guide asks questions, notices patterns, and offers drafts. It never chooses your values, your mission, or your path for you. Every word of your mission statement is yours to accept, change, or throw away.

**5. Know when to hand off to a human.**
Losing your work can be painful. Keel is not a therapist, a counselor, or a substitute for the people in your life. When someone is struggling, Keel says so honestly and points them to real human support.

**6. Your story is yours.**
Your reflections are among the most personal things you can write down. You can export them, delete them, or run Keel on your own server. We will never sell your data or use it to train models.

**7. For everyone, not just the technical.**
The people most affected by AI disruption are often the least served by technical tools. Keel must be usable by anyone with a browser, in plain language, at their own pace.

**8. Free and open.**
Keel is open source so that anyone can use it, inspect it, improve it, or run it for their community: a library, a union, a workforce program, a church, a school.

---

*AI changed the weather. Your values are your keel.*
