# Keel app (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Every screen is a place to get something done: onboarding, Where you are, Steady ground, What matters, Your mission, Support.
Audience: anyone whose work was hit by AI, often on a phone, often in a low moment. Job: get steady, then find what matters. Central theme set by the founder: **Peace**. The user should feel peace using Keel.
Constraints: WCAG 2.2 AA, plain language, manifesto voice unchanged, standard web controls, React + Vite + Convex, no UI libraries.
Memorable moment: the sun rising over still water as you answer, fully risen at the acknowledgment.
Open: per-stage horizon art beyond rise height; logo redraw beyond recolor.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Keel is public works for displaced workers, told the way the WPA told it: flat layered sunrise over still water, and peace. It refuses the calm-wellness default (cream ground, rounded white cards, soft serif) and the clinical portal.

OWN-WORLD: Flat silkscreen layers, no gradients or texture. Dawn: pale sky ground, apricot haze, gold sun, sage ridge, deep teal water. Night: deep teal ground, moon-gold sun. Big Shoulders for short display; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all reading. Actions live in the water, below the waterline.

STORY: The visitor feels unhurried and safe, understands one thing per screen, sees help is always one tap away, and leaves steadier with one next step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: a one-line tester strip; a 104px horizon band with the wordmark left and "Talk to a person" right in the sky; the question large on the sky ground with at most four options per group; Back, Skip, Next fixed in a deep-water bar at the bottom.
